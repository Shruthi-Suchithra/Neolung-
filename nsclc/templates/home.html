Why You Should Trust Us? Get Know About Us!

Welcome to NeoLung, a smart and innovative platform designed to support early detection of lung cancer—when it matters most.

Lung cancer remains one of the leading causes of cancer-related deaths worldwide, and early detection can significantly improve outcomes and save lives. Traditional diagnostic methods often identify lung cancer only in later stages, making timely intervention difficult. Our mission is to change that.

Health Care Solutions

Pulmonary

Pulmonary diagnosis for NSCLC involves imaging tests like CT and PET scans to find abnormalities, followed by a biopsy (using a needle, endoscope, or surgery) to collect a tissue sample for microscopic examination and definitive diagnosis. The biopsy results are crucial for confirming the presence of cancer and determining its specific type.

Why Choose Us

Powered by advanced technology, NeoLung uses intelligent machine learning techniques inspired by research that combines Genetic Algorithms (GA) and Support Vector Machines (SVM) to analyze biological patterns linked to Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC). This approach focuses on identifying key gene expressions that may signal early cancer risk—especially in individuals who may not show typical risk factors, such as non-smokers.
